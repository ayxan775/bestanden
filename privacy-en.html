Privacy Policy

For the iPhone app “Bestanden: DTZ B1 Prüfung” and this website.
Last updated: October 2026

This is a translation. The German version is binding.

1. In short

  • The app needs no account. It shows no ads. It uses no tracking and no analytics tools.
  • Your progress, your letters and your voice recordings stay on your iPhone. We do not receive them.
  • All learning content is inside the app. This version downloads no content from the internet.
  • The app sends data only to Apple (buying “Pro”) and to the service RevenueCat (checking the purchase). The connection to RevenueCat starts only when you open the purchase screen or restore purchases, or if Pro was bought on this iPhone.
  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics tools.

3. Data on your iPhone

The app stores this data only on your iPhone:

  • learning progress: answers, mock exam results, flashcards, practice time, an interrupted mock exam so you can continue it
  • your exam date and your study plan
  • settings, e.g. language and appearance
  • drafts of your letters
  • your error reports (see section 6)
  • whether Pro is unlocked

This data is not sent to us. We have no access to it.

Once RevenueCat has started (see section 5), its component stores a random identifier and your last purchase status on the iPhone. It also reads the iOS identifier for vendors. Storing and reading this information is strictly necessary to buy, restore and unlock Pro (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, the German law implementing Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive). No consent is required for it. If you never open the purchase screen and never restore, this does not happen.

Deleting: if you delete the app, all of this data is deleted. You can also delete error reports in the app’s settings.

Backups: depending on your iOS settings, Apple includes app data in your iCloud or device backup. Apple’s settings and privacy policy apply to that.

4. Microphone and voice recordings

  • The app uses the microphone only in the speaking exercises and in the Sprechen part of the mock exams, and only when you tap record.
  • iOS asks for your permission first. You can change it at any time in the iOS Settings.
  • The recording is saved as a file in the app’s cache on your iPhone so you can listen to it. It is not uploaded and not sent to us or anyone else.
  • There is no speech recognition and no automatic assessment.
  • iOS clears the cache by itself. The recordings are gone at the latest when you delete the app.

5. Buying Pro

Apple App Store

Apple handles the purchase: Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland. Apple processes your Apple ID and payment data under its own responsibility. We receive neither your name nor your payment data. More: apple.com/legal/privacy

RevenueCat

To check whether Pro was bought and to restore purchases, we use RevenueCat, Inc., 1032 E Brandon Blvd #3003, Brandon, FL 33511, USA.

The app connects to RevenueCat only when you open the purchase screen (e.g. by tapping Pro content) or tap “Restore purchases”. On the purchase screen it loads the product and price. When you buy or restore, it sends Apple’s purchase data. If Pro was bought on this iPhone, the app checks the purchase status at every start, so that a refund, for example, is taken into account. If you never open the purchase screen, the app sends nothing to RevenueCat. Apple also reports purchases and refunds directly to RevenueCat. The following is processed:

  • a random identifier that RevenueCat creates when it first connects (no name, no email)
  • the iOS identifier for vendors (“identifierForVendor”). It is different for every app provider. It is not the advertising ID.
  • purchase and transaction data from Apple, e.g. product, price, time, App Store country
  • technical information: device model, iOS version, app version, preferred languages
  • IP address

Purpose: to unlock Pro, to check and restore purchases and to take refunds into account.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the Pro purchase contract) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in reliable purchase checks).
Processor: RevenueCat processes the data on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR).
Transfer to the USA: based on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR), which are part of our agreement with RevenueCat.
Retention: as long as we use RevenueCat. When the agreement ends, the data is deleted. At your request we delete it earlier (see section 12).
More: revenuecat.com/privacy

You do not have to provide this data. Without the connection to RevenueCat, however, you cannot buy or restore Pro. Everything else in the app also works without internet.

6. Error reports

In the app you can report a mistake in a task. The report is stored only on your iPhone. It contains: date, reason, task number, your text (if you write one), language, app version and content version. It contains no device identifier.

The app never sends reports by itself. Only when you tap “Share” in the settings, iOS opens the share menu. You choose how and to whom you send the text, e.g. by email or messenger. That service’s privacy policy applies. Section 7 explains what happens to reports you send to us.

7. Contact by email

If you write to us, including error reports from the app, we process your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use it to reply and to correct mistakes in the content.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if it concerns your purchase. Otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering requests and offering correct content).
Retention: we delete the message when your request is done, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
Email provider: our mailbox is hosted by Gmail, a service of Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The basis for the transfer to the USA is the European Commission’s adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). More: Google’s privacy policy

8. Statistics and crash reports from Apple

If you have turned on “Share With App Developers” in iOS, Apple gives us aggregated usage statistics and crash reports. We cannot identify you from them. Apple is responsible for this. You can turn it off in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

9. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. The app opens it in your browser when you tap “Datenschutz”, “Impressum” or “Support” in the settings.

When you open a page, GitHub technically receives your IP address and information about your browser. According to GitHub, it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub does not state a retention period for this. We ourselves receive no logs and no statistics.

The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics tools and loads no fonts or scripts from other servers.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in providing the website securely and free of charge).
Transfer to the USA: GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The basis is the European Commission’s adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR).

10. Content updates

All learning content is inside the app. This version of the app downloads no content from the internet. The app has a function for later content updates (new tasks, corrections, audio). It is switched off in this version. Before we switch it on in a new app version, we will extend this policy.

11. What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics tools, no advertising ID
  • No selling of data
  • No profiling and no automated decisions under Art. 22 GDPR
